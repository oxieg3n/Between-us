# Incident response and report-operator handoff — not staffed

Prepared 2026-11-16. No live safety operator/coverage, emergency service, response promise or incident drill is established. Public report intake/promises remain off. Synthetic queues and assigned staff fixtures are technical test evidence only. Do not publish support addresses/hours until the owner names a monitored operator and approves the actual procedure.

## Handoff fields requiring named approval

Accountable operator/legal entity; privacy/security and child-safety leads; restricted on-call/backup roles; hours/timezone/holiday coverage; severity definitions and achievable response targets; safe contact channel; staff training/vetting/conflicts; jurisdiction-specific reporting/preservation duties; lawful disclosure authority; appeal owner; retention/holds; provider escalation contacts; incident/breach deadlines and safe notice approvals. All remain unapproved placeholders, not an invitation to send real reports.

The case system permits a reporter and an assigned independent active safety/privacy operator. A guardian/family admin, reported person or unassigned staff account must not acquire case data. Verification operators and consent operators have distinct scopes. A service key is not a substitute for binding an actual authenticated operator and reviewing their authorization.

## Planned confidential case workflow

1. Show an age-appropriate explanation of who receives the report, actual coverage/limits, minimum evidence and legal confidentiality limits. No automatic journal/draft/conversation attachment. Safety information and another safe adult remain accessible without guardian approval or enrollment.
2. Before accepting real reports, run a synthetic end-to-end drill: reported guardian, unsafe notification channel, operator absent/revoked, stale credentials, legal hold/deletion conflict, overdue response and emergency-risk content. No live child content is used to test operations.
3. A trained restricted reviewer triages deliberately submitted evidence and chooses a safe response channel. Do not automatically tell the alleged unsafe adult, family administrator or ordinary contact that the child reported/seeks help. Push previews contain no case identifiers, names or status.
4. Document the lawful and child-safe action, assigned reviewer, due time and follow-through using minimal metadata. Preserve only justified evidence under finite restricted holds. Escalation to external authorities/humans requires the applicable approved legal/safety procedure; this document does not perform any communication or promise dispatch.
5. Provide the actual approved appeal/status path. Closing a technical case is not proof of remedy or full deletion. Withdrawing ordinary consent must not weaken chat/finance authorization; the narrow own historical case-status surface is separate.

## Planned security incident workflow

Contain: identify scope without broad content collection; stop unsafe admission and worker delivery; quarantine direct Data API RPC and Edge paths as needed; revoke affected managed sessions/device/operator authority. Enrollment off alone does not disable existing authenticated access. Public/offline safety information remains available.

Preserve: protect necessary forensic evidence and reviewed logs under restricted, time-bounded custody. Do not copy journals, plaintext credentials, full report queues or private exports into tickets, analytics, chat or CI artifacts. Local journals are not a server incident evidence source to upload automatically.

Assess: independent security/privacy/child-safety reviewers determine affected categories/subjects/providers and applicable legal duties/current deadlines. Investigate nullable authorization, stale sessions, external guardian overlap, block/delivery races, raw publication, token/credential leakage, hidden finance inference and restored deleted data. A generic outage is not evidence of a breach; lack of an alert is not proof of no exposure.

Recover: fix and independently retest the concrete boundary before restoring access. Rotate compromised credentials through the owner's secret manager; do not disable Auth hooks/RLS/block checks. Reconcile provider queues and backups/tombstones, then use the [rollback/recovery procedures](DEPLOYMENT_AND_ROLLBACK.md). Only authorized safe notices may be sent by the actual operator; no messages were sent by this work.

Complete: record the factual timeline, scope, actions/reviewers, residual risk and follow-up deadlines without sensitive payloads. Rehearse again and attach evidence before operational launch. No severity response time, statutory deadline or 24/7 capability is invented here.
