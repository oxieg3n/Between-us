# Cost ceilings and graceful limits

Prepared 2026-11-16. Authorized new hosted/provider spend remains **$0**: local synthetic development only. No hosted project, paid plan, SMTP/push account, signing service or production deployment is provisioned here. Existing-machine disk/compute/network and engineering time still have costs; this is not a promise that a future service is free.

Use [cost analysis](../COST_ANALYSIS.md) as a dated comparison, then recheck official account/provider quotas at any owner-approved selection. Do not turn an advertised allowance, alert email or provider-specific spend cap into a universal invoice ceiling. Fixed compute, backup/PITR, extra projects, egress, database scans, identity/email/SMS, realtime and artifact storage may be separate dimensions.

## Current enforceable scope

- Work remains local, isolated and fictional; no automatic paid upgrade or fallback provider. Public enrollment/real-child/report/push gates default closed.
- The inactive CI template requires owner installation/workflow permission and manual quota acknowledgement, one concurrent standard Ubuntu job and a 40-minute timeout. No artifact upload/package cache/native signing job is configured. This caps one job's duration, not the account's dollar bill; dispatch still requires actual free-usage/budget review.
- SQL has bounded requests/pagination and message/notification budgets; workers have bounded leases/retries/time windows. Child skips, reports, journals and hidden finance activity never justify engagement/analytics collection or extra notification fan-out. Counters are operational limits, not emotional scores.
- Model downloads are explicit pinned assets, not cloud inference. No paid AI endpoint, automatic financial transfer or SMS channel is introduced to bypass current gates.

## Owner decision before any hosted staging

Record allowed provider/plan/project/region; reviewed monthly ceiling and no-upgrade policy; exact included dimensions; resource caps/admission quotas; expected synthetic load; measured per-request/connection/scan/egress cost; log/backup/export retention and storage ceilings; billing alert recipients; stop/degrade owner and response coverage. Verify hard enforcement separately from alerting. No numeric provider charge ceiling is approved until the target account is inspected and the owner explicitly authorizes it.

Proposed alert bands for review are 50/80/95% of each approved allowance, with admission throttling before exhaustion; these alerts/automation are not implemented or staffed. A safe limit returns an honest unavailable/retry state, pauses new invites/expensive work and suppresses delivery attempts. It must not expose hidden parent matching through child-specific quota errors, lose committed financial history, retry external sends forever, weaken consent/verification or silently switch to paid resources.

Measure only necessary content-free operational data under the privacy review: request/worker result codes, bounded aggregate usage, queue age, provider acceptance/receipt class and rate-limit health. No message bodies, journal/prompts, report text, credentials, device tokens, child names or private financial amounts in metrics/logs. Staff still need an approved minimal monitoring and incident plan; no monitoring service is configured by this runbook.

Stop criteria: unreviewed invoice/upgrade prompt, unexpected credentialed network target, budget/retention breach, unbounded retries/egress, missing operational coverage or inability to establish provider limits. Keep the service gated and return the exact decision to the owner; do not add a credit card, upgrade or weaken security to restore a green dashboard.
