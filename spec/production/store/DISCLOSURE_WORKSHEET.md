# Data and policy disclosure worksheet

Prepared 2026-11-16. **Unsubmitted and incomplete.** Reconcile the exact final native/web artifacts, managed Auth, database, API, notification/email providers, operational access and retention before completing store questionnaires. Server persistence means this is not a blanket “no data collected” product. Lack of advertising or analytics does not remove functionality data from the inventory.

Apple requires the developer to account for its own and integrated partners' collection and keep declarations current; functionality data can still require disclosure. Its label definition and optional exceptions need review for each actual flow. [Apple App Privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/). Google requires accurate app-wide Data safety declarations, including relevant SDK behavior, alongside a privacy policy. Apply its current collection/sharing definitions and exceptions rather than copying Apple's answers. [Google Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469).

Resolve under-13 parental rights and local journal constraints with qualified counsel. [Internal legal register](../CHILD_PRIVACY_COMPLIANCE.md).
- [ ] Actual in-app and external account-deletion request paths, strong reauthentication, independent review for disputed guardianship and precise retention exceptions. Show executed purge evidence across domains, providers/backups and tombstones. `access_revoked`, partial export or a requested job is not completed account/data deletion. [Lifecycle contract](../PRIVACY_LIFECYCLE_API.md).
- [ ] Complete current Apple age-rating/Kids Category and Google target-audience/Families/UGC/child-safety questionnaires from actual intended younger audience. Store categories/ratings do not determine legal age eligibility or replace VPC. Do not label the app 13+ merely to avoid the intended under-13 review.
- [ ] Reconcile final permissions/SDK manifests, privacy declarations, data linked to an identity, tracking/advertising absence, security claims and encryption/export-compliance answers with reviewers. Do not assume SQLCipher alone answers either store's export-compliance form.
- [ ] Verify final platform features, physical-device support, actual delivery and confidential operator capacity against listing text; screenshots fictional, current and consent-safe. No promise of live support until staffed and rehearsed.
