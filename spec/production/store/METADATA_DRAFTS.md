## Candidate full description

BetweenUs

when it's hard to find the words, start here

BetweenUs helps families make room for conversations and practice everyday household money skills.

A guardian sets up each child or teen account together with them. Each person has their own sign-in. Young people can choose what they want to say, prepare their own words, and review a message before sending it to an accepted, verified contact. Child contacts also require guardian approval. Safety information and asking another safe adult for help do not depend on parental approval.

Optional Open the Door check-ins offer a starting question. Answer, come back later or skip. There are no scores or penalties for skipping. Check-ins are available when you open the app; background notification delivery is not promised.

Household money tools help families discuss balances, goals and agreements. They record household activity; they do not open bank accounts, move money or provide financial advice.

On qualified native devices, personal writing stays in the author's local journal. A guardian's administration tools do not unlock that journal or unsent drafts. Sharing requires a separately prepared message and an explicit review. Device or key loss can permanently lose local writing. Device support, recovery limits and privacy rights must be explained before use.

BetweenUs is a communication and learning tool. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis or emergency monitoring.
