# Open the Door persistent check-ins

2026-11-16. Source: `services/backend/supabase/migrations/202611160010_checkins.sql.pending`, `supabase/tests/checkins.test.sql`, and `apps/mobile/src/production/Checkins.tsx`. Staged for root review/serialized migration execution. Synthetic accounts only; public enrollment and real child data stay closed. No worker, push provider, native build or real notification delivery was activated by this slice.

Schedules, invitations and private choices are server-persisted. Separately written response text stays in component memory until the author deliberately enters the root conversation review flow. No journal/draft text is read, copied into an answer, stored in a check-in table or published. Both children and adults can answer Little Conversations in their own words.

## RPCs and authorization

All ordinary RPCs derive the caller from the current active managed session via `communication_actor()`; role metadata, client clocks and author IDs are not authority. Tables are private, RLS-enabled, with no raw authenticated writes/selects and no Realtime publication. Schedule and invitation operations prelock the sorted union of family IDs for the relevant relationships before any pair/row locks. The batch app-open check uses the external-contact-aware `privacy_lock_families` helper supplied by migration 008.

Guardian edits bind the question's creator to the editing guardian and require that guardian's own permitted contact. Recipient changes require a separate schedule. Revision conflicts reject with `22023`; sender/client UUIDs bind exact logical saves/removals and changed replays reject. An edit invalidates old revision invitations. It does not generate a second invitation on the same local day, even after a skip or edit. Normal future recurring dates remain independent optional invitations, without escalation.

The server uses PostgreSQL timezone conversion to compute due instants. A repeated local date can generate only one occurrence; no client time or `now` argument exists. Physical timezone/DST/sleep-resume interaction qualification remains necessary. Opening the screen is the implemented trigger; do not advertise a background scheduler or scheduled push until a separately operated worker and actual device/provider tests pass. Questions must never enter a generic push payload if that worker is added.

## Mobile integration

Export `ProductionCheckins({context,onBack,onPrepareConversation?})`. Callback payload is exactly `{recipientId,text,response:'listen'|'reply'|'talk'}`. The screen first calls the ID-only preparation RPC; then passes the author's separately edited, deliberately chosen text to the callback. Root must revalidate the connection when composing/reviewing and again on Send. This component never invokes `send_message` or treats preparation as delivery.

The UI reuses approved Open Letter components, supports guardian create/edit/remove and recipient/day/time/timezone preview, and offers child quick choices, longer own response, private postpone and skip. Quick choices fill an editable answer; they do not send it. Little Conversations requires an explicitly selected current peer. Without the callback, preparation is disabled and the screen states that review is not connected. Unsaved words are memory-only and cleared on leaving; postponing/skipping with written words asks before discarding them.

The workspace is keyed by account UID. Every `accountRpc` supplies the captured `context.user.id` as its third expected-user argument, preventing an old account's UI intent from dispatching under a newly signed-in account before a React update. Responses are also checked against the current workspace generation. Uncertain saves retain their exact command/UUID for retry and pause navigation/new mutation; confirmed rejection releases the form. No role selector or guardian impersonation is added.

Safety information and seeking another safe adult remain root-shell/public/offline capabilities, independent of check-in consent, login, connection or guardian approval. This screen must not hide that existing path.

## Privacy lifecycle hooks

The private `checkin_own_export(subject)` helper and BEFORE INSERT/UPDATE `privacy_lifecycle_jobs` trigger augment an actual self-export artifact. Guardians receive only schedules they authored, never child invitations/choices or aggregate response indicators. Children receive their own invitation identifiers/dates/private choices. The child export deliberately contains no otherwise inaccessible question body. The helper is not granted to clients and accepts no user-controlled role. It adds no report, journal or finance data.

When the reviewed deletion executor reaches `access_revoked`, the same transaction cancels schedules authored by or addressed to the subject, and actually erases the subject's own unheld invitation/choice rows. Creator/recipient account or message holds preserve relevant bytes while access remains revoked. The executor already holds the sorted family/pair locks; the hook does not acquire a new lower family after a relationship lock. A precise held-checkin blocker is appended only to internal operator review; public generic shared/held blockers and `deletionComplete:false` stay intact. A parent deletion never exports or erases another child's private choice merely because they authored the calendar.

Service-only `run_checkin_retention(p_subject_id uuid,p_operator_id uuid,p_limit int default 100)` requires an independent privacy operator and approved lifecycle/retention policy. It actually removes expired own invitations when neither participant is held, expires command receipts after 30 days when unheld, and removes inactive calendars older than 30 days once no invitation references/holds remain. Invitations expire after the local occurrence date plus eight days; only explicitly deferred older invitations are shown in that window. These are synthetic proposed settings pending the full retention/legal/operations review. No full backup/provider/device purge is claimed.
