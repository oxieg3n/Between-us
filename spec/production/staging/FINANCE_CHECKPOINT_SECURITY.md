## Conservative design under review

V1 proposes moving only visibly settled, bounded connected components of occurrences and payouts, connected by payment lots and original public allocation source maps, out of hot collections. Private matching does not veto visible archival or consume visible capacity. Live private records remain in the validated book; eligible fully invalidated records use a separate private archive and bounded economic summaries. The complete immutable event and operation archive remains authoritative. Net paid cents may be folded into validated state; earned cents and correction obligations retain their existing meaning. An authorized old correction rehydrates the exact component and original allocation provenance, removes its folded paid contribution exactly once, applies the unchanged engine, then either archives the newly settled result or retains a bounded reopened component. Large or visibly dependent components remain hot and existing limits still apply. This is deliberately narrower than unlimited financial history.

Security acceptance requires a precise definition of settled and connected, not a caller-supplied flag. Start with the occurrence/earning and include every payout/payment/source edge needed by any member. A payout funded by several earnings pulls all those earnings into the closure; an earning paid through several payouts pulls all those payouts into it. Positive free, allocated or held source lots veto archival of their component. Acknowledgment/correction actions must remain exactly addressable after archival; they must not disappear because an item left hot state. Zero lots/allocations may be pruned only when used IDs, prior eligibility, reversal state and matching allocation/source lineage remain intact. Component membership and all reference facts are derived from trusted state and cross-checked before publication.

**Visible eligibility is essential.** A hidden-match dependency must not make one child-visible world retain hot records while an otherwise equivalent world archives them. That would eventually change capacity admission, history shape or errors. Root's revised strategy keeps private matching records outside component eligibility: after occurrence hydration, the existing engine can invalidate the retained private matches by source. Historical zero/released/automatic allocation paths therefore require equivalent-world tests and exact retained source/allocation identities, rather than a hidden-state-dependent veto. A private-match count, private checkpoint budget or private-reference overflow must not become a child-visible capacity oracle.

Private matches can grow through ordinary allocations even without another parent matching command. The existing hidden-command limit does not itself bound that derived collection. Checkpoint validation, matching projection and old reversal must therefore be measured under matching-heavy archive cycles. Visible collection compaction alone is not proof of bounded lifetime memory or runtime. An explicit unresolved limitation must remain if indexed retained facts or another validated bound cannot satisfy the realistic-family requirements.

The implemented candidate addresses that growth with private capsules only when every source is fully invalidated, active cents equal funded cents, correction-due cents equal funded cents, and no positive free/allocation/held/payment lot references the original allocation ID. Transfers preserve that original ID. Per-goal active/funded/revealed-funded totals and current family-local-month usage stay in bounded summaries; exact parent reveal/reconciliation hydrates the private record and subtracts its summary once. An old month's record cannot debit the new month's usage. Original public source maps exist even when matching is off; zero allocations remain while referenced, and their complete source closure can keep a partially paid component hot in both worlds.

Public worst-case provenance, rather than actual private records, is charged against the book/family capacity envelope. The candidate accounts for possible matching records from public source keys and maximum-width values, rules, goal summaries and bounded private parent activity. The original verified migration003 ceiling was64MiB for aggregate JSON. A provisional uncommitted4MiB checkpoint choice did not fit the conservative twelve-child reserve and was replaced by that existing64MiB ceiling; this is not a measured hosted-memory pass or proof that4MiB fits. The pure twelve-child fixture is4,139 actual bytes with14,861,399 potential bytes. Root still owns actual Node/Edge/database capacity measurement and the release disposition.

## Required invariants

| Boundary | Acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Archive authority | Original accepted events, actor-bound command UUID/fingerprint rows and used entity IDs remain immutable. Compaction does not become another money event or replace the accepted sequence with hot-array length. A mutable active pointer may advance only through validated CAS; prior archive/capsule records remain intact. |
| Current identity | Every read, correction, history page, checkpoint publication and export uses current managed session/account/consent/family authority. Hold/replay cursors, archived IDs or knowledge of a checkpoint never grant authority. Keep the established session-before-family lock order and post-wait checks; document admitted in-flight semantics. |
| Scope | Every capsule/entity/reference lookup is bound to the authoritative family and child. Operation identity keeps the existing `(family, actor, operation UUID)` namespace and binds child plus logical fingerprint. Same UUID in another valid family is a separate namespace; it must never retrieve or replay the first family's result. |
| Atomic state | Event/command insertion, hot state, folded totals, reference facts and active checkpoint head agree at one accepted revision. Append/checkpoint/correction races have one winner or bounded conflict/retry, never a partial published combination. A checkpoint cannot skip an accepted event in its declared stream. |
| Exact money | Rehydration does not re-credit earned cents or duplicate correction obligations. Subtract only that capsule's folded net paid contribution once before loading its payment objects. Engine corrections retain integer cents, payment acknowledgment/reversal, source eligibility and matching rounding behavior. |
| Reopened capacity | `reverse_payment` on a valid earning can recreate held/free lots. Reserve bounded hot capacity before publishing the correction, or reject with zero mutations and an honest bounded-capacity result. Never drop overflow, partially apply a correction or report success while deferring untracked money changes. |
| Independent old references | An indexed exact reference can find original occurrence reward/reversed state, payment-to-payout/source lots and immutable command identity after many checkpoints. An ID cannot be reused because its entity left hot state. Do not load all family history to answer an ordinary request. |
| Shared records and holds | Closing an account revokes access and expires artifacts; it does not delete another author's shared finance or override finite reviewed holds. Existing `deletionComplete:false`/generic shared-record blockers remain until a separately reviewed financial purge policy exists. A released hold is not permission to bypass append-only triggers. |
| Resource boundary | New archive/checkpoint/reference writes participate in database resource guards and bounded request/response/work limits. Keep critical access revocation available. Free storage is finite; immutable archive preservation does not justify an unlimited-storage, unlimited-component or automatic paid-upgrade claim. |

For old corrections, retain private indexed typed references such as `(family, child, entity kind, entity ID)` and source edges such as `(family, child, earning ID, target kind, target ID)`, or an equivalent reviewed structure. Required facts include original reward and reversal state; payment/payout linkage, original/net/reversed/acknowledged amounts and source lots; prior allocation eligibility; and retained private match source shares, original eligible base, invalidated shares, funding/reconciliation and family-local month usage. Private matching stays outside visible capsule eligibility and DTOs. The current engine's `reverse_earning` and `reverse_payment` explicitly depend on these facts. Scalar balance summaries are insufficient.

One old earning can have many historical dependents. A bounded component limit must be real: reject archival before exceeding it and preserve the component hot. If a future version handles unbounded fan-out, use correct cumulative accounting or a reviewed private resumable/shadow-state workflow with one atomic publication. `LIMIT` followed by ignoring remaining edges and synchronous full-history replay are not bounded correct alternatives. This V1 must state which normal financial workflows still reach existing caps; the internal longevity gate closes only to the extent the owner's actual requirements are met.

## Integrity and corruption handling

Use maintained SHA-256 implementations and the existing deterministic canonical JSON/fingerprint rules; no application-designed encryption, signature or MAC protocol is needed. At minimum bind the checkpoint format/engine version, family/child/timezone/roster scope, accepted sequence/revision boundary, previous validated source identity, exact component member/reference manifest and complete checkpoint content. The authoritative database checks scope/CAS and recomputes canonical digests rather than accepting an unrelated caller's expected hash. Ordered archive membership must account for interleaved events; a component need not itself occupy consecutive family sequence numbers, but the declared checkpoint stream cannot omit accepted input.

A matching hash is not proof of correct monetary semantics or a defense against an administrator who can rewrite both the content and trusted expected digest. Preserve the explicit trusted finance-service computation boundary. The trusted engine derives the next checkpoint from a validated predecessor and the exact bounded accepted tail, with structural and money/reference invariants checked. An untrusted candidate/import/repair blob must never become the authoritative head merely because its caller also supplies a matching recomputed hash. Any stronger defense against a compromised service credential or database superuser requires a separate design and is not claimed here.

Reject wrong scope/version, unknown fields, unsafe integers, unsupported shapes, inconsistent folded/reference totals, missing/duplicate/gapped source membership, mismatched hashes and stale/future boundaries. Keep the old valid state unchanged; return a stable generic unavailable result for an affected scope. Do not reset to empty balances, regenerate missing facts, silently fall back to unbounded replay or expose corruption details to a child. Preserve minimal private operational evidence. Repair is an explicitly authorized trusted procedure that reconstructs from immutable authoritative records, validates the result and publishes through CAS; it is not an ordinary guardian/child API. Other unaffected scopes and static safety help should remain usable.

## Role, history and privacy surfaces

Raw archive/checkpoint/reference tables require RLS with no ordinary client grants, empty definer search paths, explicit execution grants and no Realtime publication. A child receives only the current typed child projection. Ordinary events such as allocations/reversals can carry hidden match effects, so excluding matching event kinds alone is not safe archive redaction. A child history response must also exclude raw effects, source lots, internal hashes/revisions/sequences, private match IDs/counts and another author's private records.

History cursors must be bounded, scope-bound and reauthorized on each page; current role or consent loss invalidates access even when the cursor was minted earlier. Cursors, `hasMore`, counts, progress, response/status/header shape, pagination order and notification/hint production must not depend on unrevealed matching. Do not expose checkpoint IDs, compaction status, elapsed private work or archival eligibility as child progress. Equivalent-world tests must include functional capacity admission, not only JSON equality. Timings are measured as bounded performance evidence; project-wide quota/contention remains a residual side channel and no constant-time certification is claimed.

The existing own-privacy flow remains requester=subject, fresh reauthentication and current requested session. Parent finance artifacts recheck current guardian scopes; closed/former scopes keep review/partial blockers rather than granting family-wide historical access. New bounded export pages/jobs must bind an exact validated financial snapshot and current authority, detect concurrent-source changes, expire consistently and preserve complete authorized content without loading an unbounded raw state in one request. No archive/checkpoint/export may include journals, unsent drafts, model sessions, guardian journal keys or confidential reports. Identity closure must not newly publish stale roster/display names in cursors or public metadata.

## Proposed adversarial integration cases

These are future tests, not results. Use fresh fictional managed identities and real local SQL/API paths with authorized positive controls. Root runs database/services serially. Exact RPC and fault-injection interfaces must be agreed before this reviewer edits integration code.

1. **Direct authority and scope:** anonymous, child, unrelated guardian, former guardian and ordinary authenticated callers cannot select raw tables or invoke private checkpoint/archive/repair RPCs. Wrong family/child/reference/cursor cannot retrieve another scope. Catalog assertions cover all new tables/functions/grants/publications.
2. **Source and checkpoint forgery:** wrong version/zone/roster/boundary, swapped family capsule, altered folded totals/reference membership, missing/duplicate rows and corrupted hashes fail with zero head/event/command mutations. A recomputed digest on an untrusted candidate still fails independently trusted source validation. Do not claim resistance to an all-powerful administrator.
3. **Publication races and crash:** actual separate connections contend on append versus checkpoint, two checkpoint publications and old correction versus fresh append. Assert one accepted state and bounded retry. Root's owned crash harness checks before-commit rollback; separate exact-operation retry covers an unknown/post-commit client outcome.
4. **Idempotency and used IDs:** retry the same immutable operation across several archive/checkpoint cycles and restore; one original accepted event remains and the response projects current authorized state. Changed body/child or reused archived entity ID fails. A valid second family remains independent and cannot replay the first family's result.
5. **Conservative closure and accounting:** many small eligible settled components archive, while a multi-earning/multi-payout component is all-or-nothing. Positive active/held/free/allocated lots and oversize components remain hot. Zero/released/automatic allocation history may compact only with preserved used IDs, private match lineage and correction/acknowledgment addressability. Compare exact earned/paid/available/held/correction cents before/after archive/rehydrate/rearchive, including a component with retained private matches.
6. **Old correction after eviction:** reverse an old paid earning, then its payment; reverse a payment before reversing its earning; retry both and test cancellation/acknowledgment. Verify folded paid is removed/re-added exactly once, eligibility never regenerates, old history stays immutable and reopened-capacity failure leaves no mutation.
7. **Hidden equivalent worlds:** matching off versus hidden configured/funded/reversed variants with identical visible actions, including historical released/automatic allocation paths. Compare child bodies/status/errors/headers, IDs/cursors/`hasMore`, activity/progress, quota admission and hints/notifications around archival thresholds. Measure bounded workload/timing separately and retain the global timing caveat.
8. **Current authority races:** session expiry during lock wait, remote session revocation, consent withdrawal, child closure, guardian removal and role/account changes deny later archive/correction/history/export work at the specified checkpoints. A stale response must not enter another author's UI or export.
9. **Exports, deletion and holds:** authorized own/guardian projections retain the same boundaries after archival; wrong subject/expired reauth/current scope loss denies. Shared financial holds and immutable history survive deletion; artifacts expire and public blockers do not disclose another person's report/hold. No falsely complete deletion result.
10. **Restore and repair:** quarantined restore retains archive/checkpoint/command/reference inventories, owners/RLS/grants and exact cents; apply independently current revocations before access. Old credentials deny, UUID replay stays single and source corruption cannot advance a repair/head. A local SQL restore is not live hosted Auth/provider recovery or independent off-site custody.

Each case records source/config/schema identity, exact synthetic positive/negative control, observed result, unperformed branches and residual limits. Earlier failed runs stay distinguishable from later passes. Bounded peer review, unit fakes and local integration are engineering evidence; independent review, hosted Edge CPU/memory, real operations, legal/privacy policy and physical qualification remain separate gates.

## Privacy export continuation

Migration029 captures exact `FinanceView.activity` entries before pruning, plus current role-safe snapshots, in the same transaction as a committed financial command. Replay/conflict does not rewrite capture. Original event ID/time/kind/actor/reference binding, immutable activity rows, canonical role-specific snapshot hashes, RLS, empty definer paths and service-only grants protect the seam. Bare legacy SQL writes remain supported for finance tests, but missing capture after archive introduction blocks export for review. Cached older hot-only ready artifacts are returned to partial review rather than silently remaining complete.

The captured source merges current hot positions, every current visible archive entity and complete role-filtered audit activity; parent sources additionally include sanitized private match records. No raw lots/effects/original allocation maps enter the artifact. Hidden filtering precedes child coverage/count/byte checks, with visible-only indexes and bounded source readers. Accepted activity chronology is retained without returning global sequence numbers. Physical capsule IDs, private hashes, matching fields and global revision do not enter the child logical stamp. Source and attach compare the same static logical data, while Root's trusted projector recomputes temporal payout and parent month counters at the source's fixed recorded time using bounded server-only hot state. That state never becomes artifact content.

Own request/subject binding, original live session, fresh reauthentication, current independent assigned operator and current guardian scope remain authoritative. A missing snapshot after any uncaptured mutation requires review because a hidden-kind reveal/reconciliation may affect visible goals; normal private-only captured changes preserve child stamp/status. Parent-only snapshot corruption is checked only on the parent path, while child snapshot corruption fails its own source. Shared immutable financial copies and holds are retained; no automatic finance purge or complete-deletion claim is introduced.

The legacy branch delegates unchanged reviewed functions only when every relevant family lacks any checkpoint/archive and remains within the original per-child128 visible/32 hidden event limits, including empty histories. No full private archive replay is used to fabricate missing projection/activity. New child artifacts above1MiB and parent artifacts above8MiB require review and cannot become ready.
