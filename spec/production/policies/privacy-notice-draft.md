## Short explanation for children and teens

Between Us is intended to help you write and choose whether to talk with people you know. You can skip a check-in. Skipping does not create a score or punishment. You do not have to send a message because you wrote in your journal.

Your journal is intended to stay on this device, under your own journal key. The proposed family controls do not give a guardian a journal key. Losing this device or key, removing the app, or some recovery steps may permanently lose your writing. Someone who can unlock your device may be able to see it. We have not approved this journal design for real children yet; legal rights requests and device recovery rules still need review.

A message is different from a journal. You compose it separately, choose the recipient and review the exact words before sending. The recipient can read and copy what you send. Blocking stops future permitted contact; it cannot make someone forget or erase a copy they already made. [Insert verified message storage, moderation and deletion visibility explanation.]

Safety information is available without signing in or asking a guardian. Between Us is not emergency help or therapy. During development, reports are practice reports using fictional details and do not reach a real safety team. [Do not replace with a staffed-support promise until approved.]
