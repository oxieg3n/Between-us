# Safety reporting and response — draft

Prepared 2026-11-16. INTERNAL CHILD-SAFETY/LEGAL/OPERATIONS REVIEW ONLY. Public report intake and staffed-support promises are OFF. Synthetic staging may submit, triage and close fictional reports with synthetic staff fixtures; no report from a real person or external notification is authorized. Technical acceptance of a report is not proof that anyone is monitoring it.

## Wording permitted during synthetic development

“Practice report — use fictional details only. This report is not sent to a real safety team and will not bring help. You can block this contact. Safety information is available without signing in or asking a guardian.”

Do not use “our safety team will contact you,” response deadlines, confidentiality guarantees or “submitted for help” in an unstaffed environment. Emergency guidance may direct someone in immediate danger to nearby safe human help or local emergency services; do not claim emergency monitoring/dispatch. No unverified regional phone directory is included here.

## Proposed release experience — cannot be published yet

Before submission, explain: [operator receiving the report], [staffed hours/timezone], [response target by severity], [safe response channel], [who can see evidence], [mandatory/legal disclosure limits], [retention], and that immediate help may require emergency services. Use child-readable wording; do not guarantee complete confidentiality or a specific outcome.

The intended audience includes younger under-13 children and teens. Guardian-managed setup together is required for every child/teen account at every age, with server-verified enrollment intent, distinct child/teen authentication, their own credentials and acceptance, and no public self-signup or ordinary guardian impersonation. Guardian product approval is separate from any legally required teen consent. This enrollment invariant does not restrict safety information or seeking another safe adult without guardian approval. Before choosing a minimum age, review whether the youngest proposed users understand block/report, evidence sharing, response limitations and unsafe-guardian help. Provide an age-appropriate access/recovery path that does not require a child's personal email/phone or an alleged unsafe guardian's credential. Review each candidate state's reporting duties for maximum US coverage; this draft has not completed that survey and no operator/counsel is named.

Require only the minimum report category, subject/message reference and optional separately chosen evidence/description. Never attach a journal, private draft, contact book or entire conversation automatically. Preview the exact evidence and recipients before submission. Explain that submitted report evidence leaves ordinary conversation controls and enters restricted case handling. No child should have to disclose journal content to get help.

Blocking and reporting are independent. A block immediately denies future permitted messaging/subscriptions/queued delivery under the server rules; reporting must not unblock. A guardian cannot remove a child's independent block. Provide report/help access when the reported person is a guardian, when consent is withdrawn, or when the account is suspended, using a reviewed minimal-data pathway.

Safety-resource access does not require collecting personal information. A real report that does collect information before consent or after withdrawal needs a separately reviewed lawful basis and minimum fields. Counsel must reconcile applicable safety exceptions, their parent-notice provisions and unsafe-guardian risks; do not use a generic “safety” label to bypass consent for ordinary account/content collection. Keep public intake disabled until that procedure is approved.

No routine report receipt, case status, push notification or guardian-admin export may reveal the child's report to the alleged unsafe person. Ask whether and how it is safe to respond. Use neutral notifications only if separately chosen and safe. A case ID is not a bearer credential. Reauthenticate and authorize every case read; restrict staff by assigned function and log access without copying sensitive payloads into ordinary logs.
