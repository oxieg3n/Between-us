# Retention, export and deletion — draft

Prepared 2026-11-16. INTERNAL REVIEW ONLY. These are proposed product defaults for synthetic testing, not legal minima, an implemented deletion guarantee or an approved public schedule. All periods require purpose/necessity approval by privacy counsel, security and operations. Public enrollment remains OFF. The approved schedule must be included in the online notice before real collection; see [COPPA retention rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.10).

Audience intent is maximum US state coverage and the youngest appropriate preteen ages alongside teens; an exact minimum/maximum age is unapproved. Guardian-managed setup together with the child/teen is required for every child/teen account, regardless of age, with no public self-signup bypass or disabling toggle. Under-13 support is a core design requirement. Distinct child/teen authentication, own credentials and acceptance remain separate from guardian product approval and legally required teen consent. Guardians cannot ordinarily impersonate a child; safety information and seeking another safe adult never require guardian approval. State eligibility and age bounds stay closed pending review, rather than using a 13+ restriction to avoid the work. Minimize eligibility records to an approved age band/transition and coarse guardian-declared state; no GPS or precise-address history. Enrollment-intent, pairing/credential/recovery records for all child/teen ages need their own purpose, finite period and safe deletion rules before collection; a child's personal email/phone is not required by default.

## Proposed schedule for review

Use the earliest applicable expiry, withdrawal/deletion trigger or loss of purpose. Do not restart retention when a backup is restored or a worker retries. No “forever for safety” or “until we might need it.”

The proposed 30-day deletion target is an engineering target, not an extension of a shorter legal duty. Final notices must describe actual request-response and completion deadlines by jurisdiction, any valid extension and appeal. Where a period cannot be approved or enforced, keep the relevant collection/feature disabled.

## Request and execution requirements

1. Provide an easy in-app account-deletion initiation and an external web request resource at release. Allow export and withdrawal independently. Explain proposed scope/irreversibility, server/device/recipient limits and specific lawful exceptions before confirmation. Reauthentication protects against malicious deletion without creating unnecessary obstacles. Do not require a phone call/email as the ordinary deletion route.
2. Verify only the necessary identity/authority. An adult account or family-admin role alone cannot export/delete another author's private writing. Refer a statutory parental request, unsafe-guardian claim or custody conflict to the reviewed restricted procedure. Do not reveal account existence to a claimant before verification.
3. On valid withdrawal/deletion, deny new affected collection and operations immediately: revoke sessions/grants as appropriate, invitations, queued sends, realtime subscriptions, scheduled work and notification tokens. Deletion completion cannot be simulated by hiding the account in the UI.
4. Execute an idempotent per-category deletion plan across primary records, derived indexes, caches, attachments, provider systems, exports and backups. Remove or de-identify shared records only where the method actually removes identifiable linkage; pseudonymous IDs and hashes can remain personal data. Do not retain a raw “deleted events” payload.
5. Journal erase requires an authenticated author-device operation with independently reviewed key/database/file cleanup and OS-backup behavior. SQLCipher encryption alone does not establish secure deletion. Unreachable/lost devices cannot be guaranteed remotely erased. Never export journal keys to a guardian to satisfy a server request. Resolve legal obligations before releasing the feature.
6. Record a minimal request ID, verification outcome, scoped category status, deadlines, exceptions and completion time without retained content. Provide a safe acknowledgement/completion channel and an appeal route. Avoid notifying the alleged unsafe guardian about a confidential request.
7. Restore backups only through a restricted process that replays deletion/revocation tombstones before serving restored data. Confirm provider expiry; prevent old push tokens, invitations and consent receipts from restoring authority. If a vendor cannot meet the approved period, change the design or stated schedule before enrollment.

## Holds and exceptions

Only a specifically identified legal duty or counsel-approved claim/security preservation need may suspend an otherwise due deletion. Record the authority, exact records, reason, restricted custodian, expiry/review date and release condition. Review proposed holds at least every 30 days; delete promptly when released. No generic “may retain for any legal reason” blanket. Counsel/child-safety must decide what notice is lawful/safe; records under hold remain unavailable for product use, model training, ordinary family access or marketing.

Release evidence: inventory-to-storage mapping, lawful request matrix, own/parent/teen/guardian-dispute exports, provider deletion contracts, expiry/hold jobs, stale queue revocation, offline-device limitations, and a full delete/restore drill using synthetic records. Each category needs a named owner and approved period. Apple/Google deletion obligations and the unresolved journal rights conflict are mapped in [child privacy requirements](../CHILD_PRIVACY_COMPLIANCE.md).
