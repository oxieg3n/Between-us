## Refreshed federal and platform sources

Read 2026-11-16. The [2025 final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule) states effectiveness June 23, 2025 and general compliance April 22, 2026, with specified safe-harbor exceptions. [Current Part312](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312) displayed Title16 current through November12,2026. Counsel must assess notice before covered collection, suitable VPC, separate non-integral disclosure consent, verified parental rights, a written security program with coordinator/assessments/supplier assurances and finite purpose-based retention. An adult's account control is distinct from guardian authority. The [FTC FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) directs readers to the amended rule; its device-stored-photo example is not a journal-specific exemption.

[Apple guidelines1.2,1.3,5.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) require UGC safeguards, reporting/response, blocking and contact information; category/parental gates need assessment. [Account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion) must be mapped to actual deletion, including retained-data exceptions. [Google Families](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335) requires truthful audience/data/SDK declarations, social safety reminders, adult management and adult action before child information exchange. That adult action is not itself COPPA VPC. [Google deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111) requires in-app and external request paths; [Child Safety Standards](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14747720?hl=en) needs separate category/applicability review and a real accountable contact.

New research issue: [NY S9051B](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9051) records signature as chapter368 on October28,2026. **Active B text section3 specifies January1,2027**; earlier versions on that page use a different effective-date formula. Counsel must assess its AI-companion definitions against optional local reflection and other state AI duties. On-device execution is not presumed exempt; no therapy/companion relationship or harmful-output claim is authorized.

## Register interpretation and completion contract

Exactly 50 states plus D.C. appear below. Every row is `notReviewed / DENY`. The source date records this engineering source-discovery pass, **not** counsel review. “Specific instruments TBD” means the required primary-statute determination is unfinished, not that the jurisdiction has no law. Legislative/enforcement portals are entry points; they do not prove applicable sections or an absence of litigation. JS, rejected and failed fetches are disclosed. No personal data was submitted and no government contact was made.

**S (scope pending), separately required for every row:** counsel determines entity/threshold/territorial reach, private messaging/social-service and optional AI classification; under-13/teen/majority bands; general/minor privacy, consumer-health, biometrics and age-assurance rules; direct notice/VPC/teen grants; custody/rights/appeal; breach, reporting and preservation duties. Add exact statute and final-rule sections, effective dates, current court docket/order with scope and retrieval date, purpose-by-purpose applicability reasoning, feature controls, permitted age/state conditions, reviewer name, signed decision and renewal/event triggers. Public feed absence, small revenue, no ads or local inference cannot alone establish exemption.

A qualified reviewer may replace a row with `reviewPending`, `approvedWithConditions` or `excludedWithReason` and attach evidence. Unknown age/state, unresolved source/order, expired review or unimplemented conditions continue to deny enrollment. The software must keep public enrollment off until a separately reviewed server policy implements the approved decisions; this Markdown register is not an executable allowlist. Territories and foreign jurisdictions are unapproved.
