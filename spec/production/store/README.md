# Store preparation — internal drafts

Prepared 2026-11-16. Nothing in this directory has been submitted, uploaded or approved by a store. Public enrollment and real-child data remain OFF. These files prepare a concrete review; they do not authorize developer-account creation, signing, spending, publishing or collection of real data.

Use the existing BetweenUs name, approved symbol and Open Letter direction. Official tagline: **when it's hard to find the words, start here**. Keep Safe Harbor light and Night Harbor dark. Use approved originals in `docs/assets/`, `apps/mobile/src/` and `design/branding/` after verifying the actual asset path; do not regenerate a similar logo. Screenshots must depict the final qualified build with fictional data and accurate feature availability. Historical prototype captures cannot establish production behavior.

- [Metadata drafts](METADATA_DRAFTS.md) are proposed words, with no availability promise.
- [Disclosure worksheet](DISCLOSURE_WORKSHEET.md) is a data inventory and unresolved submission checklist, not completed App Privacy/Data safety declarations.
- [Release gates](../RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) and [device qualification](../SUPPORTED_DEVICES.md) control readiness.
- [Child privacy review](../CHILD_PRIVACY_COMPLIANCE.md) and [draft policies](../policies/privacy-notice-draft.md) remain subject to qualified review.

The owner still must approve the legal operator/contact address, privacy/support/account-deletion URLs, app category, store target-age bands, rating questionnaire, youngest appropriate age and age-transition policy, evidence-backed US state eligibility, pricing, release territories and signing custody. Broad US coverage and younger users are product goals; there is no approved nationwide rollout or exact age range. Guardian-managed setup with the child/teen is mandatory for every child/teen account, including older teens. Store age ratings are not consent or legal eligibility decisions.

No placeholder URL or unstaffed mailbox may be submitted as a working service. No synthetic operator may be described as a staffed safety team. Do not claim end-to-end encryption, privacy from everyone, guaranteed deletion/recovery, live notifications or 24-hour monitoring. Physical Android/iOS and native sign-in callbacks remain unverified. The final store review must reconcile the actual binary, services, policies and operational capacity on the same release date.
